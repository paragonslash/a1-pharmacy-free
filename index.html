Repeat prescriptions

Your repeats, quietly handled

Nominate us once and your prescriptions arrive here on their own. We chase the surgery when something is missing, and synchronise the dates so everything lands together.

Nominate A1 Order a repeat
  1. 01

    Nominate us

    Tell your GP surgery that A1 is your pharmacy, or do it here in about a minute. Electronic repeats then arrive with us automatically.

  2. 02

    We dispense and check

    A pharmacist checks every item against your record — interactions, doses, and anything that has quietly stopped making sense.

  3. 03

    Collect or we deliver

    A text when it is ready. Collect from the counter, or free delivery locally and nationwide in two to three days.
