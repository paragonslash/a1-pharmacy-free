The analgesic to reach for first. Effective for mild to moderate pain and for bringing a temperature down, and safe alongside most other medicines — which is exactly why the dose limit matters.

Note from the pharmacist

The most common accidental overdose we see comes from taking paracetamol tablets and a cold and flu sachet together. Check the label of everything else in the cupboard first.

Composition
Paracetamol
500mg · Per tablet
Directions

Adults and children over 16: one or two tablets every four to six hours. Maximum eight tablets in 24 hours.

Warnings and cautions
  • Never exceed eight tablets in 24 hours. Overdose causes serious liver damage, sometimes with no early symptoms.
  • Do not take with any other paracetamol-containing product, including cold and flu remedies.
  • Seek immediate medical advice in the event of an overdose, even if you feel well.
Delivery and returns
