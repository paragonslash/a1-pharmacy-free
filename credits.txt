IMAGE CREDITS
=============

The images in this download are GENERATED PLACEHOLDERS. They match the
dimensions of the photography used on the demo site so that every layout
renders at the correct proportions, but they are not photographs and carry no
licence restrictions of their own.

Replace them with your own photography, or with images you have licensed, before
publishing.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THE REAL PHOTOGRAPHY IS NOT INCLUDED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The photography on the live demo is licensed for display on that demo only. It
is not licensed for redistribution inside a template that other people download
— which is a separate, commercial use — and the portraits are of identifiable
people, which additionally requires a model release.

Rather than ship images we cannot grant you rights to, the download contains
placeholders at identical dimensions. Nothing reflows when you swap yours in.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT GOES WHERE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

  images/products/     1100 x 1100   Packshots, square, shot on white
  images/people/        900 x 1200   Staff portraits, 3:4
  images/editorial/    varies        Full-bleed interior / counter photography
  images/journal/      varies        Article header images, landscape

Filenames are referenced directly in the HTML. Keep them, and keep the aspect
ratios, and no markup or CSS needs to change.
